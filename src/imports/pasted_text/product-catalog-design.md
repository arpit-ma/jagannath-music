Important Design Instructions

* Use only realistic, high-quality product photography.
* Do not use cartoons, illustrations, or generic stock graphics.
* Build reusable product card components.
* Maintain consistent spacing, typography, and visual hierarchy.
* Use modern hover effects and micro-interactions.
* Design all sections fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Use realistic placeholder products, descriptions, and prices for demonstration purposes only.
* Do not include fictional testimonials, fake reviews, fake ratings, fake sales figures, or fabricated business achievements.
* The final design should look comparable to premium music equipment retailers and professional audio gear stores.
